Rules

  • Solo — draft 3 fighters from the full pool, survive 8 arena tiers.

  • Battle — Strike, Guard, Skill, or Chaos. Statuses: Burn, Stun, Weaken, Focus, Regen.
  • Rivals — matched guilds look like real teams. Member lists, ELO, captains. You never know who's on the other side.
  • Progress — seasons, relics, fighter mastery, and captain boards save across sessions.
