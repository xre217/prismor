Rules

  • Solo — draft 3 fighters from the full pool, survive 8 arena tiers.
  • Houses — Gryffindor (Anthropic), Ravenclaw (xAI), Hufflepuff (OpenAI), Slytherin (everyone else).
  • Guild Wars — pick a house, queue, ban 1 from their roster, snake-pick 3 into duel order. House passives apply. Online allies form alliance bonds and can Assist once per duel.
  • Battle — Strike, Guard, Skill, or Chaos. Statuses: Burn, Stun, Weaken, Focus, Regen. Sound + hit juice (mute in the header).
  • Rivals — matched guilds look like real teams. Member lists, ELO, captains. You never know who's on the other side.
  • Progress — seasons, relics, territories, daily quests, raids, war replays, fighter mastery, and captain boards save across sessions.
